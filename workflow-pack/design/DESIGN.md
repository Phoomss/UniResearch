## Brand & Style

The UniResearch design system is based on the concept of a **Modern Digital Library with Human Warmth**. It combines the reliability and organization of an academic repository with a friendly visual personality suitable for students, lecturers, researchers, and university staff.

The design should feel welcoming, intelligent, calm, and thoughtfully crafted. It must move away from the cold atmosphere of traditional academic databases without becoming childish or overly playful.

The visual style is called **Editorial Minimalism with Tactile Softness**. It uses warm paper-like backgrounds, structured layouts, restrained colors, generous spacing, and subtle interactions. The interface should feel closer to a contemporary academic publication or carefully designed university library than a generic technology startup.

The overall emotional response should be:

* Clear and easy to understand
* Calm and comfortable to read
* Friendly without losing credibility
* Distinctive without being visually loud
* Modern without relying on design trends
* Cute through thoughtful details rather than decoration

Avoid generic purple SaaS layouts, neon gradients, excessive glow effects, random floating shapes, and repeated card patterns that make the interface look automatically generated.

## Colors

The color identity is named **Mulberry Library**.

The palette is inspired by book covers, warm paper, editorial publications, library interiors, and soft academic stationery.

### Primary Color — Deep Mulberry

Use `#603F83` as the primary brand color.

Deep Mulberry communicates creativity, knowledge, and academic confidence without feeling as common or artificial as bright digital purple.

Use it for:

* Primary buttons
* Active navigation items
* Important links
* Input focus states
* Selected filters
* Primary icons
* Key headings or small headline accents
* Important interface actions

Do not use Deep Mulberry as a large full-page background.

### Secondary Color — Scholarly Periwinkle

Use `#526DAB` for accessible secondary actions and `#7895D1` for decorative or supporting accents.

Periwinkle communicates trust, exploration, and digital knowledge.

Use it for:

* Secondary buttons
* Informational icons
* Category illustrations
* Search suggestions
* Research metadata highlights
* Kinetic Grid active nodes
* Supporting interface states

Avoid strong Mulberry-to-Periwinkle gradients across large surfaces.

### Accent Color — Soft Apricot

Use `#F4A978` as a restrained warm accent.

Apricot adds friendliness, warmth, and a memorable visual identity to the cooler Mulberry and Periwinkle palette.

Use it sparingly for:

* Featured research labels
* Small notification indicators
* Highlighted keywords
* Selected category details
* Small decorative nodes in the Kinetic Grid
* Important but non-critical emphasis

Apricot should occupy approximately 2–5% of the visible interface.

Do not use Apricot for long text, standard body copy, or large backgrounds.

### Main Background — Warm Paper

Use `#FCFBF8` instead of pure white for the main page background.

Warm Paper reduces visual harshness and creates a reading experience similar to a modern printed publication.

Use pure white `#FFFFFF` for:

* Research cards
* Search containers
* Navigation surfaces
* Modal windows
* Dropdown menus
* Important content areas

### Tonal Surfaces

Use tonal surfaces to separate content without excessive borders or shadows.

* Soft Lavender: `#F1EDF7`
* Powder Blue: `#EAF1FA`
* Apricot Wash: `#FFF0E6`
* Neutral Container: `#F1EDF3`

Use these colors for:

* Alternate page sections
* Category cards
* Filter panels
* Statistics icon containers
* Featured research areas
* Selected states
* Informational panels

Do not assign a completely different color to every card.

### Text Colors

* Primary text: `#262434`
* Secondary text: `#706D7B`
* Muted or disabled text should be derived from the secondary color with reduced emphasis.

Use the main text color for:

* Page headings
* Research titles
* Navigation labels
* Important information

Use the secondary text color for:

* Authors
* Advisors
* Dates
* Categories
* Descriptions
* Metadata
* Supporting instructions

Avoid using low-contrast pastel text for essential information.

### Color Distribution

Use approximately:

* 70% Warm Paper and white surfaces
* 20% neutral, lavender, and powder-blue containers
* 8% Mulberry and Periwinkle
* 2% Apricot accents

Color must always communicate hierarchy, interaction, category, or status. Do not use color only as decoration.

## Typography

The typography system follows a **Thai-first bilingual strategy**.

### Thai Typography

Use **Kanit** for all Thai interface text.

Kanit provides clear Thai character forms, modern geometry, and strong readability for digital interfaces.

Recommended weights:

* Display and hero headings: 600–700
* Section headings: 600
* Button and label text: 500–600
* Body text: 400
* Metadata: 300–400 when contrast remains sufficient

Thai text should generally be displayed 5–8% larger than equivalent English text to achieve similar optical weight.

Use generous line height to prevent Thai vowels and tone marks from appearing crowded.

### English Typography

Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** for all English interface text.

It complements Kanit through its rounded geometry and friendly semi-formal tone.

### Typography Principles

* Use no more than three noticeable font weights within one section.
* Avoid excessive bold text.
* Keep research titles to a maximum of two lines in cards.
* Use sentence case instead of all-uppercase English labels.
* Do not use uppercase styling for Thai text.
* Maintain a comfortable reading line length of approximately 55–75 characters for long content.
* Keep metadata compact but never smaller than a comfortably readable size.

## Layout & Spacing

Use a centered, responsive container with a maximum width of `1280px`.

The desktop layout should use a 12-column grid. The mobile layout should use a 4-column grid.

### Alignment

The design must feel balanced and intentionally symmetrical.

Use:

* Equal left and right page margins
* Consistent card widths
* Aligned headings and content edges
* Predictable grid gaps
* Balanced negative space
* Consistent internal card padding
* Clear visual anchors

Avoid random asymmetry or decorative offsets that do not support usability.

### Spacing Rhythm

All spacing follows a 4px base unit.

Primary spacing values:

* 4px for tightly related micro-elements
* 8px for icons and labels
* 16px for related content groups
* 24px for standard card padding and layout gaps
* 40px for subsection spacing
* 64px for major section spacing
* 96px for large desktop page transitions

Use at least 64px of vertical spacing between major desktop sections.

On mobile:

* Use 20px side margins
* Reduce major section spacing to 48–64px
* Preserve at least 16px between cards
* Avoid reducing typography excessively to fit content

## Elevation & Depth

Use elevation sparingly.

The design should rely primarily on tonal contrast, borders, spacing, and hierarchy rather than large shadows.

### Standard Card Shadow

Use:

`0 8px 24px rgba(38, 36, 52, 0.06)`

### Hover Shadow

Use:

`0 12px 32px rgba(96, 63, 131, 0.10)`

### Floating Elements

For dropdowns, popovers, and modal windows, use:

`0 16px 40px rgba(38, 36, 52, 0.10)`

### Interaction Behavior

On hover:

* Move interactive cards upward by no more than 2px
* Slightly strengthen the border or shadow
* Do not scale cards dramatically
* Avoid bounce animations

Transitions should normally last between 150ms and 250ms.

## Shapes

The shape language should feel soft, mature, and consistent.

Avoid applying extremely rounded corners to every component, as this can make the interface look like a generic AI-generated design.

### Recommended Radius

* Small badges and compact controls: 8px
* Buttons and input fields: 12px
* Standard cards: 16px
* Featured containers and modal windows: 20px
* Large hero or CTA surfaces: 24px
* Pills only for tags, status labels, and compact filters

### Iconography

Use rounded line icons with consistent stroke width.

Icons should:

* Use simple shapes
* Remain recognizable at small sizes
* Avoid unnecessary detail
* Use one coherent visual style
* Support text rather than replace important labels

Do not mix filled, outlined, 3D, and cartoon icons in the same interface.

## Kinetic Grid

The hero section may use a subtle **Kinetic Grid** representing connected knowledge and academic discovery.

### Grid Colors

* Primary grid lines: `#DED6EC`
* Secondary grid lines: `#D8E4F3`
* Active nodes: `#7895D1`
* Occasional accent nodes: `#F4A978`
* Background: `#FCFBF8`

### Grid Behavior

The grid should:

* React gently to mouse movement
* Use restrained parallax depth
* Move slowly and smoothly
* Fade toward the hero edges
* Stay behind all text and controls
* Never reduce text contrast
* Include only a small number of active nodes
* Be simplified or disabled on mobile
* Respect reduced-motion accessibility preferences

Avoid cyberpunk aesthetics, bright glowing lines, dense particles, or gaming-inspired movement.

## Components

### Buttons

#### Primary Button

* Background: `#603F83`
* Text: `#FFFFFF`
* Minimum height: 46–48px
* Radius: 12px
* Horizontal padding: 20–24px

Hover:

* Use a slightly darker Mulberry tone
* Move upward by no more than 1px
* Add a restrained shadow

Focus:

* Use the defined focus ring
* Do not remove the browser focus state without replacement

#### Secondary Button

* Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#F1EDF7`
* Border: `#7895D1`
* Text: `#52326F`
* Radius: 12px

Hover:

* Use `#EAF1FA` or a slightly stronger border

#### Tertiary Button

* Transparent background
* Mulberry text
* Clear hover underline, arrow movement, or subtle surface state

Do not use icon-only buttons when the action may be unclear.

### Cards

Research cards should use:

* White background
* `#E8E5EC` or `#D2CBD7` border
* 16–20px radius
* 24px internal padding
* Minimal standard shadow

Research card content hierarchy:

1. Category or research type
2. Research title
3. Short description
4. Author and advisor
5. Academic year and metadata
6. Tags and actions

Keep cards within the same row visually consistent in height.

Do not give each card an unrelated pastel color.

### Input Fields

Use:

* White or very light neutral background
* `#D2CBD7` border
* 12px radius
* Minimum height: 48px
* Clear placeholder text
* Visible labels above fields when needed

Focus state:

* Border: `#603F83`
* Focus ring: `rgba(96, 63, 131, 0.14)`
* Background remains white

Do not rely only on placeholder text as the field label for complex forms.

### Search Bar

The homepage search bar is the primary interaction and should be visually prominent.

Use:

* White background
* Soft border
* Mulberry search button
* Clear search icon
* Visible text label on the action button
* Comfortable horizontal and vertical padding

Placeholder example:

“ค้นหาชื่องานวิจัย ผู้จัดทำ อาจารย์ที่ปรึกษา หรือคำสำคัญ”

Search suggestions may use Soft Lavender and Powder Blue backgrounds.

### Chips and Tags

Use pill shapes only for:

* Research topics
* Categories
* Status labels
* Search filters

Recommended styles:

* Lavender background with Mulberry text
* Powder Blue background with accessible Periwinkle text
* Apricot Wash background with deep Terracotta text

Avoid highly saturated backgrounds.

Limit cards to two or three visible tags before showing a “+ more” indicator.

### Lists

Research lists should use:

* 16–20px vertical padding
* Subtle dividers
* Clear title hierarchy
* Consistent metadata alignment
* Visible hover and keyboard focus states

Do not place every list item inside a heavy card.

### Navigation

Use a sticky navigation bar with:

* Warm Paper or translucent white background
* Soft lower border
* Mulberry active state
* Ink-colored inactive links
* Clear language switcher
* Distinct Submit Research action

Avoid strong glassmorphism, excessive blur, or glowing borders.

### Bilingual Toggle

Use a compact segmented control for:

* ไทย
* EN

The selected language should use:

* Mulberry background
* White text

The unselected language should use:

* Transparent or Soft Lavender background
* Ink or Mulberry text

Do not use flags to represent interface languages.

### Statistics

Statistics should feel calm and academic.

Use:

* Ink-colored numbers
* Dusty Gray labels
* Lavender or Powder Blue icon containers
* Mulberry or Periwinkle icons
* Apricot for only one small highlight when necessary

Avoid giant numbers, glowing icons, and aggressive analytics-dashboard styling.

### Featured Research

Use a Soft Lavender or neutral surface.

Recommended styling:

* Deep Mulberry heading
* White document preview
* Periwinkle border or supporting illustration
* Apricot featured badge
* Balanced split layout

Do not use a full saturated gradient background.

### Footer

Use a light footer background such as `#F5F2F7`.

Use:

* Ink-colored headings
* Dusty Gray body text
* Mulberry links
* Periwinkle hover states
* Clear column alignment

Avoid a large black or dark-purple footer unless required by the brand context.

## Interaction & Motion

Interactions should feel soft, intentional, and predictable.

Recommended duration:

* Hover feedback: 150ms
* Standard component transition: 220ms
* Section or panel transition: 300–350ms

Use motion for:

* Hover feedback
* Focus transitions
* Loading states
* Expanding filters
* Search suggestions
* Bookmark state changes
* Kinetic Grid movement

Avoid:

* Bounce effects
* Large scaling
* Continuous decorative movement
* Excessive parallax
* Animated gradients on every element

## Accessibility

The interface must meet at least WCAG AA contrast expectations.

Ensure:

* Normal text has sufficient contrast
* Focus states are visible
* Interactive controls have a minimum target size of approximately 44px
* Status is not communicated through color alone
* Thai text remains readable at all breakpoints
* Body text is not excessively light
* Reduced-motion preferences are respected
* Icons have accessible labels when they do not include visible text
* Error messages clearly explain how to resolve the problem
* Disabled states remain identifiable and readable

## Final Design Principle

UniResearch should feel like a real university product designed with care.

The final experience should combine:

* Academic credibility
* Thai-friendly typography
* Warm editorial character
* Clear usability
* Restrained visual personality
* Balanced symmetry
* Consistent component behavior
* Subtle and mature cuteness
* Modern interaction design
* A memorable Mulberry, Periwinkle, and Apricot identity

Every color, surface, shape, and motion decision must have a clear functional purpose.

The interface should never look like a generic AI-generated SaaS landing page.
